# Glossary: 3D NAND Memory Hole Etch

Terms are defined as they are used in this book. Chapter references point to the main discussion.

---

## A

**A (local aspect ratio):** Depth divided by mean hole width, A = z/w̄. Used in the transport and ARDE models. 80 at the bottom of the reference hole. (Ch. 1.3.1)

**A₀:** The aspect ratio at which the ARDE etch rate has fallen to half of R₀. 80 in the reference process; higher is better. (Ch. 3.4)

**Acceptance angle (θ_c):** Largest angle from the hole axis at which an ion can reach the bottom without striking the wall, about 1/A. 12.5 mrad (0.72°) at A = 80. (Ch. 3.2.2)

**ALD (Atomic Layer Deposition):** Self-limiting conformal deposition used for the blocking oxide, trap nitride, tunnel oxide, and channel films in the hole. (Ch. 1.2)

**Amorphous carbon (a-C):** The hard mask for the hole etch. 1.8 µm in the reference. (Ch. 11.1)

**APC (Advanced Process Control):** Feed-forward and feedback adjustment of recipes using metrology and tool data. (Ch. 15.4)

**ARDE (Aspect-Ratio-Dependent Etching):** Fall in etch rate with increasing aspect ratio. Modeled as R = R₀/(1 + A/A₀). Also called RIE lag. (Ch. 3.4)

---

## B

**Blind hole:** A hole that does not reach the bottom landing layer, usually from a particle, clogging, or insufficient overetch. (Ch. 1.6, 12.2)

**Blocking oxide:** First film deposited on the hole wall, preventing back-tunneling of charge. (Ch. 1.2.1)

**Bow (wafer):** Out-of-plane deflection of the wafer from stack stress, about 150 µm for the reference stack. (Ch. 2.3)

**Bowing (hole):** Local widening of the hole sidewall at some depth, caused by off-axis ions and neutral etch. Spec ≤ +4 nm in CD. (Ch. 10.3)

---

## C

**Channel hole:** Alternative name for memory hole. (Ch. 1)

**Charging (differential):** Positive charge at the hole bottom and negative on the mask top, because the hole shadows isotropic electrons but not directed ions. Deflects ions and causes twisting. (Ch. 3.5)

**Child–Langmuir sheath:** Collisionless space-charge-limited sheath. Thickness s = (√2/3) λ_D (2V_s/T_e)^(3/4). (Ch. 5.3)

**Clausing factor (W):** Probability that a molecule entering a tube reaches the far end, with no sticking. W ≈ 1/(1 + 3L/(8R)). (Ch. 3.3.1)

**Clogging:** Closing of the hole by passivation at the etch front, which stops the etch. Also called pinch-off or etch stop. (Ch. 11.4)

**CMP:** Chemical mechanical planarization, used at the multi-deck joint. (Ch. 14.2)

**Core fill:** Oxide that fills the hole after the channel film; the core must be at least about 20 nm. (Ch. 1.2.1, 10.4)

**CuA (CMOS under array):** Architecture with periphery beneath the array. (Ch. 1.5)

**Cryogenic etch:** Hole etch at wafer temperatures of about −60 to −30 °C, typically with HF chemistry. (Ch. 4.5, 8)

---

## D

**Deck:** One stack and its etched hole. Multi-deck (string-stacked) flows etch two or more decks. (Ch. 14)

**Dummy hole:** Hole added outside the active array so the outermost active holes see the same environment as interior ones. (Ch. 13.4.2)

---

## E

**Edge ring (focus ring):** Consumable around the wafer that shapes the sheath at the edge; its height sets edge tilt. (Ch. 5.4)

**Ellipticity:** (CD_max − CD_min)/CD_mean. Spec ≤ 3%. (Ch. 10.5.1)

**E_req:** Mean ion energy needed for a target pass fraction, E_req = 2T_⊥ ln[1/(1−f)] A². (Ch. 6.1)

**ESC (electrostatic chuck):** Holds the wafer by electrostatic force and cools it with backside helium. (Ch. 8.4)

**EWMA:** Exponentially weighted moving average, used in run-to-run feedback. (Ch. 15.4.2)

---

## F

**f_pass (pass fraction):** Fraction of ions that reach the hole bottom. 46% at A = 80 and 4 keV. (Ch. 3.2.2)

**Faceting:** Sputter erosion of the mask corner at 45–70° incidence, which grows the top CD. (Ch. 11.3)

**FDC (fault detection and classification):** Real-time monitoring of tool signals. (Ch. 15.5)

---

## G

**g_f:** Net lateral growth rate at the etch front, per side. Sets taper. 0.5 nm/min in the reference. (Ch. 10.2)

---

## H

**HF (hydrogen fluoride) chemistry:** Cold etch chemistry in which HF adsorbs on the surface and, with low wall sticking, reaches the hole bottom. (Ch. 4.5)

**HV-SEM:** High-voltage scanning electron microscope that sees hole bottoms through the stack. (Ch. 15.2)

---

## I

**IED (ion energy distribution):** Distribution of ion energies at the wafer; bimodal for low-frequency sinusoidal bias. (Ch. 5.2, 6.3)

**Ion transverse temperature (T_⊥):** Sets the angular spread, σ_θ = √(T_⊥/E). About 0.5 eV. (Ch. 3.2.1)

---

## J

**Joint:** Interface between decks in a multi-deck stack, including a thick oxide layer and the plug landing. (Ch. 14.3)

**Johnsen–Rahbek ESC:** Chuck with a weakly conducting dielectric giving stronger clamping at a given voltage. (Ch. 8.4.1)

---

## K

**Knudsen transport:** Gas motion in a tube where molecule–wall collisions dominate over molecule–molecule collisions. (Ch. 3.3)

---

## L

**Landing layer:** Polysilicon or SEG base on which the hole etch stops, 120 nm in the reference. (Ch. 2.5.1, 12.3)

**λ (decay length):** Depth over which a neutral's density falls by 1/e, λ = D/√(3s). (Ch. 3.3.2)

---

## M

**Memory hole:** Vertical cylindrical hole etched through the stack, in which the cell films and channel are formed. (Ch. 1)

**Mask open:** Etch of the carbon hard mask before the hole etch. (Ch. 11.1)

**Microloading:** Dependence of local etch rate on local pattern density. (Ch. 13.4)

---

## O

**OCD (optical critical dimension):** Spectroscopic scatterometry that gives array-averaged CD, depth, and taper. (Ch. 15.2)

**ON stack:** Alternating oxide/nitride stack for replacement-gate 3D NAND. (Ch. 2.1.1)

**ONO:** Oxide–nitride–oxide cell film stack (blocking, trap, tunnel). (Ch. 1.2.1)

**OP stack:** Alternating oxide/polysilicon stack for gate-first designs. (Ch. 2.1.2, 4.6)

**Overetch:** Etch beyond nominal depth to cover the tail of the depth distribution; 164 nm (66 s) in the reference. (Ch. 12.2)

---

## P

**Pair pitch (p):** Thickness of one oxide + nitride pair. 60 nm in the reference. (Ch. 2.1.1)

**Passivation:** Polymer or oxide film on the sidewall that protects it from lateral etch. (Ch. 4.4)

**P/T (precision-to-tolerance):** 6σ of the measurement divided by the specification range. (Ch. 15.2.1)

**Pulsed bias:** Bias power applied in pulses, relieving charging and reducing average ion flux. (Ch. 6.4)

---

## R

**R₀:** Etch rate at A → 0 in the ARDE model; 300 nm/min in the reference. (Ch. 3.4)

**Recess:** Depth of the hole bottom into the polysilicon landing layer, 30–60 nm. (Ch. 12.3)

**Replacement gate:** Flow in which nitride layers are removed and replaced with tungsten word lines. (Ch. 1.2.2)

---

## S

**S (selectivity):** Ratio of etch rates; for the mask, the ON depth divided by mask loss; for the landing layer, S_Si is the ON rate divided by the poly rate. (Ch. 11.2, 12.3)

**SEG (selective epitaxial growth):** Silicon grown at the hole bottom as the base of the channel. (Ch. 12.4.2)

**Seasoning:** Waferless deposition of polymer on chamber walls to remove the first-wafer effect. (Ch. 9.2)

**Sheath:** Space-charge region between the plasma and the wafer; about 1.2 cm at several kV. (Ch. 5.3)

**Stoney equation:** Relation between film stress, thickness, and wafer curvature. (Ch. 2.3.2)

**String stacking:** Multi-deck architecture. (Ch. 14)

---

## T

**Taper:** Narrowing of the hole from top to bottom, about 20 nm per side (0.14°) in the reference. (Ch. 10.1)

**Tilt:** Angle of the hole axis from vertical; set by ion angle at the edge. Spec ≤ 0.1°. (Ch. 5.4, 13.2)

**Twist:** Random lateral wandering of the hole axis with depth. Spec ≤ 3 nm rms. (Ch. 13.1)

---

## W

**WAC (waferless autoclean):** Cleaning plasma run with no wafer to remove polymer from the walls. (Ch. 9.1.2)

**Web:** Material between adjacent holes; 76 nm in the reference at the bow depth. (Ch. 16.2)
